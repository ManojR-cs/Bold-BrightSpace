# Professional Package Deliverables Audit

Audit date: 2026-10-10
Source: client-provided “Interior Design Website — Website Development & Pricing” image, Professional Package card, recommended site outline, optional add-ons, and project terms.

This audit separates the pricing sheet's package promises from implementation evidence. A website section is not counted as a separate page. “Verified” means inspected in the current repository or exercised in the desktop browser; source review and external setup are identified separately.

## Package contents extracted from the pricing sheet

**Professional — ₹12,000–₹15,000+:** 6–8+ pages; Business package plus individual project pages, project filtering, a before/after gallery, advanced sections/animations, enhanced SEO, and extended support.

**Business package inherited by Professional:** Home, About, Services, Projects, Contact, WhatsApp, click-to-call, project gallery, testimonials, Maps, enquiry form, responsive design, basic SEO, and 2 revision rounds.

**Recommended site-outline content shown on the sheet:** Home (hero, introduction, featured projects, services, why choose us, testimonials, consultation CTA); About (company story, experience, design philosophy, team); Services (residential, commercial, modular kitchen, bedroom, living room, renovation); Projects (categories, photos, location/type, descriptions, before/after where available); Contact (phone, WhatsApp, email, enquiry form, Google Maps, social links).

## Pricing-sheet conditions and scope conflicts

- **Professional price shown:** ₹12,000–₹15,000+. This repository does not verify the agreement, invoice, or payment status.
- **Payment:** 50% advance and 50% before final deployment. Payment receipt/status is outside the codebase and unverified.
- **Timeline:** approximately 5–7 working days after required content and images are received. The receipt date/completion schedule is unverified.
- **Domain and hosting:** third-party costs are separate unless specifically included. The domain and hosting target are still marked NEEDS CONFIRMATION in project context.
- **Content and imagery:** client-provided logo, project photos, and business information are preferred. The repo contains project photos, but project groupings and other missing business facts remain unconfirmed.
- **Revisions:** two rounds are included in Business; Professional says it includes Business. No revision log or written acceptance record is in the repository.
- **Optional add-ons:** extra standard page (₹1,000–₹1,500/page); individual project detail page (₹1,000–₹2,000/project); advanced filtering (₹1,500–₹3,000); before/after slider (₹1,000–₹2,000); extra revision round (₹500–₹1,000); monthly maintenance (₹1,000–₹2,000/month); additional major feature quoted separately.
- **Clarifications needed:** Professional includes individual project pages, filtering, before/after gallery, and extended support, while the add-on list separately prices individual project pages, advanced filtering, a before/after slider, and maintenance. The included count/level and the boundary of extended support are not defined.
